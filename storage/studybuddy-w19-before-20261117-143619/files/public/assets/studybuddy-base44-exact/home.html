For schools

A school layer that knows its own boundaries.

Bring StudyBuddy into classrooms without losing control of your data. Onboarding is guided, permissions are explicit, and each school works inside its own walls.

Organization-scoped by design

Every staff view is limited to its own school. One school can never read another's records.

Roles you can actually explain

Owner, school admin, teacher and learner — each with a defined set of permissions.

Deny by default

Access is refused unless a rule explicitly allows it, and student records are never public.

Personalised, gently

Built around one learner, not one average.

No leaderboards, no comparison engines. Personalisation here means the shelf remembers what you loved and the dashboard remembers what you were doing.

Goals that stay small

A weekly target the learner sets themselves — three sessions, one chapter, one new world. Progress fills quietly in the corner.

Folders for everything made

Finished work is kept, not discarded. Learners build folders for a subject, a project, or a private collection.

Pick up where you left off

Recently used apps and saved activities sit on the dashboard, so returning never starts from a blank page.
